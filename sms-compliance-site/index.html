SMS Opt-In Instructions

SEFS employees and approved internal operations contacts may opt in to Southeast Flooring Solutions job intake and operations SMS messages by texting START or YES to +1 931-404-9990.

By texting START or YES to +1 931-404-9990, recipients agree to receive job intake, scheduling, follow-up, and internal operations SMS messages from Southeast Flooring Solutions. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help. Reply STOP to opt out.
